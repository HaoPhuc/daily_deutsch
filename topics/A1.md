# A1 topics

Grammar (G) is introduced in order, one topic each Saturday. Vocabulary themes (W) run in order, about one every two weeks.

## Grammar
- **A1-G01** Pronunciation and the alphabet
- **A1-G02** Personal pronouns and *sein*
- **A1-G03** Present tense of regular verbs, *haben*
- **A1-G04** Numbers, prices, time and dates
- **A1-G05** Word order: verb second, W-questions, yes/no questions
- **A1-G06** Gender, definite and indefinite articles, plural
- **A1-G07** Accusative
- **A1-G08** Negation: *nicht* and *kein*
- **A1-G09** Present tense of irregular verbs (vowel change)
- **A1-G10** Modal verbs
- **A1-G11** Separable verbs
- **A1-G12** Possessive articles
- **A1-G13** Dative basics: *mit, bei, nach, zu, von* and dative pronouns
- **A1-G14** Perfekt (intro): *haben/sein* + Partizip II

## Vocabulary themes
- **A1-W01** Greetings and introducing yourself
- **A1-W02** Family
- **A1-W03** Food and drink
- **A1-W04** Shopping
- **A1-W05** Time and daily routine
- **A1-W06** City and directions
- **A1-W07** Home
