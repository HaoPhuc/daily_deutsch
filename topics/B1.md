# B1 topics

Grammar (G) is introduced in order, one topic each Saturday. Vocabulary themes (W) run in order, about one every two weeks.

## Grammar
- **B1-G01** Adjective declension in full
- **B1-G02** Relative clauses
- **B1-G03** Präteritum of all verbs (for reading and stories)
- **B1-G04** Passive (present and past)
- **B1-G05** Konjunktiv II: wishes and hypotheticals
- **B1-G06** *zu* + infinitive
- **B1-G07** *um … zu* and *damit*
- **B1-G08** Two-part connectors: *entweder … oder, weder … noch, sowohl … als auch*
- **B1-G09** Verbs with prepositions, *da-* and *wo-* forms

## Vocabulary themes
- **B1-W01** Study and university life
- **B1-W02** Jobs and applications
- **B1-W03** Media
- **B1-W04** Environment
- **B1-W05** Giving opinions and arguing
