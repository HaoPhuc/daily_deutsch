# A2 topics

Grammar (G) is introduced in order, one topic each Saturday. Vocabulary themes (W) run in order, about one every two to three weeks.

## Grammar
- **A2-G01** Perfekt in full: irregular and separable verbs, *sein* verbs
- **A2-G02** Dative in full: articles, pronouns, verbs with dative
- **A2-G03** Two-way prepositions (*in, an, auf* … with accusative or dative)
- **A2-G04** Comparative and superlative
- **A2-G05** Subordinate clauses: *weil, dass, wenn*
- **A2-G06** Reflexive verbs
- **A2-G07** Präteritum of *sein, haben* and modal verbs
- **A2-G08** Imperative
- **A2-G09** Future with *werden*
- **A2-G10** Polite Konjunktiv II: *würde, hätte, wäre, könnte*
- **A2-G11** Adjective endings (intro)

## Vocabulary themes
- **A2-W01** Work
- **A2-W02** Travel
- **A2-W03** Health
- **A2-W04** Housing
- **A2-W05** Free time
- **A2-W06** Appointments and plans
